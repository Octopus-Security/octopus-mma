Muay Thai footwork is more measured than boxing footwork. The basic rule is step-and-follow: the foot nearest the direction you want to go moves first, and the other foot follows by the same distance, so the stance is the same shape at the end of every step. The second basic move is the pivot, turning on the ball of the lead foot to take an angle on the opponent. The animation shows the forward and backward step-and-follow; the pivot is a turn toward or away from the camera, so it cannot be drawn in side view.

## Steps

1. From your stance, push off the rear foot and slide the lead foot forward a half step, keeping it low.
2. Let the rear foot follow by exactly the same distance, so the stance is restored.
3. To retreat, push off the lead foot and step the rear foot back a half step first, then let the lead foot follow.
4. To pivot, keep your weight on the ball of the lead foot and swing the rear foot around in an arc (a quarter turn is plenty), turning the hips and shoulders with it.
5. After every step or pivot, check that the stance has the same width and the guard has not moved.

## Key Points

- The foot nearest the direction of travel moves first; never cross the feet.
- Small steps. A half step keeps you balanced and ready to kick or check at any time.
- Keep the weight on the rear leg as you step, so the lead leg is still free to check.
- The pivot is on the ball of the lead foot, and the whole body turns together.
- Moving backward in a straight line is how fighters get trapped; step back and then pivot off the line.

## Common Mistakes

- Stepping with the wrong foot first, so the stance narrows or the feet cross.
- Taking big steps that leave you too wide or too narrow to kick.
- Bouncing high on the toes like a boxer, which makes the low kick easy to land on you.
- Dropping the hands while concentrating on the feet.
- Pivoting on the heel, which is slow and twists the knee.

## Drills

- Line drill: step-and-follow forward the length of the room, then back, checking stance shape after every step.
- Call-and-move: a partner calls "forward", "back", "pivot"; you move once and freeze in stance.
- Step-jab: step forward with a jab landing as the lead foot lands, then follow with the rear foot.
- Pivot off the ropes: back up two steps, then pivot left to get off the line.

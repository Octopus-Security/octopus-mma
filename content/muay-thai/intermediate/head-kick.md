The head kick is the body kick thrown high, and it is one of the most dangerous strikes in the sport. The Thai name means a kick to the neck, which is where the shin lands: across the side of the neck and the jaw. Because the target is higher, everything has to turn further: the step out is wider, you rise up on the ball of the lead foot, and the hips turn all the way over so the leg swings up without bending at the knee. It is usually set up, because a head kick thrown cold is easy to see coming. The animation shows the guard, the step out and turn, the impact, the return, and the guard.

## Steps

1. From the guard, step the lead foot out at an angle, landing on the ball of the foot.
2. Rise up on the ball of that foot and start to swing the rear leg; the hips begin to turn.
3. Turn the hips all the way over and swing the leg up, nearly straight, so the shin lands on the neck and jaw.
4. Swing the same-side arm down and back for balance; keep the other glove on your own face.
5. Bring the leg back the way it came and put the foot back under you in the guard.

## Key Points

- The hips do the work. The leg follows the hips up; it is not lifted by the thigh.
- Rise on the ball of the lead foot and pivot it, heel turning toward the opponent. A flat foot cannot turn far enough.
- The leg is close to straight at impact. Strike with the lower shin, not the foot.
- Keep a glove on your own face: the head kick leaves you open to a counter.
- Set it up: a jab, a cross, or body kicks first so the guard comes down.

## Common Mistakes

- Kicking higher by bending the knee and flicking, which lands with the foot and has no power.
- Not stepping out, so the hips cannot turn over.
- Leaning back so far that you fall away from the kick.
- Dropping both hands as the leg goes up.
- Throwing it without a setup.

## Drills

- Hip mobility: slow controlled leg swings to head height before every session.
- Slow-motion head kicks on the heavy bag, only as high as the hips allow.
- Pads: the holder holds the pad at head height; body kick, body kick, head kick.
- Jab, cross, head kick on pads.

The double leg is the most widely used takedown in wrestling and in MMA. It puts every fundamental together: change levels, penetrate deep with the shoulder to the opponent's hips, pull both legs in by the backs of the knees, then step up and drive forward until they fall. The power comes from the legs and the drive, not from lifting with the arms. The animation shows both people at shooting range, the level change, the penetration step with the hands behind the knees, the step-up and drive, the opponent tipping over, and the finish on top.

## Steps

1. Start in stance at shooting range: close enough that one penetration step reaches the opponent's hips.
2. Change levels: the hips drop, the head and chest stay up.
3. Penetration step: the lead knee comes down between or just outside their feet, your shoulder into their hips (around the belt line), both hands reaching behind their knees.
4. Keep your head tight to their side, against the ribs, not in the middle of their body.
5. Step the rear foot up beside you, pull their knees into your chest and drive forward with the legs, head pushing through.
6. They tip over backward. Come down with them, still holding the legs.
7. Finish with your chest on their hips, ready to move to the side and control.

## Key Points

- The shot is won by the level change and the penetration step. If those are right, the finish is easy.
- Head to the side and up, pressing into their ribs. Never put your head down in the middle.
- Pull the knees in tight to your chest; their legs should not be able to move back.
- Drive forward through the opponent, using the legs and turning a little if needed. Do not try to lift them.
- Keep driving until they are down. Stopping halfway gives them time to sprawl.

## Common Mistakes

- Shooting from too far away, so you land in front of them and get sprawled on.
- Reaching for the legs with the arms while the feet stay behind.
- Head down and eyes on the mat, which gives up the front headlock and guillotine.
- Stopping on the knees after the step instead of stepping up and driving.
- Gripping around the calves or ankles instead of behind the knees.

## Drills

- Double-leg entries: level change, penetration step, hands behind the knees, then back to stance. The partner stays standing.
- Shot to finish on a crash pad, the partner letting themselves be taken down.
- Sprawl and shoot: one partner shoots, the other sprawls, then both reset and switch roles.
- Reaction shots: the partner steps forward or reaches, and you shoot on their movement.

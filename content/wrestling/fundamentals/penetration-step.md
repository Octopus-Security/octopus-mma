The penetration step is how a level change becomes a shot. The lead foot takes a long, low step deep between or beside the opponent's feet, the rear leg drives, and the lead knee comes down to the mat while your hips keep moving forward. The word "penetration" matters: the step goes through the opponent, not up to them. It is drilled on its own until it is automatic, then built into the double leg, the single leg and the high crotch. ## Steps

1. Start in stance and change levels: hips drop, head and chest stay up.
2. Take a long, low step with the lead foot, pushing hard off the ball of the rear foot.
3. Let the lead knee come down to the mat, roughly where the opponent's feet are.
4. Keep the rear leg driving, the back straight and the head up: you are still moving forward when the knee lands.
5. In the drill, drive off the lead foot back up into stance and repeat. In a takedown the rear leg steps up instead.

## Key Points

- Level change first, then the step. The hips go down before they go forward.
- The step is long and low. Your shoulder needs to reach the opponent's hips.
- The knee lands softly on the mat. It does not crash down; the rear leg is still pushing.
- The back stays straight and the head up the whole time, so you drive into the opponent instead of diving under them.
- Keep the elbows in; the hands go behind the knees in the real shot.

## Common Mistakes

- Reaching with the hands while the feet stay behind.
- A short step, which leaves you on your knees in front of the opponent instead of underneath them.
- Bending at the waist and looking at the floor.
- Slamming the knee into the mat. Use knee pads, and drill the soft landing.
- Stopping when the knee lands. The rear leg should still be driving.

## Drills

- Penetration steps down the mat: step, knee down, rear foot comes up beside it, back into stance, repeat across the room.
- Alternating-leg steps, so both sides are comfortable.
- Partner penetration: step in to the partner's hips with the shoulder touching, without taking them down.
- Shadow wrestling: stance and motion, level change, penetration step, back to stance.
